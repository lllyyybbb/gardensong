Each poem is assembled by a Markov chain from public-domain poetry — James Weldon Johnson’s The Book of American Negro Poetry (1922) and the complete poems of Paul Laurence Dunbar. Humidity sets the length; temperature sets the width and the wildness. The same reading always writes the same poem.
