gardensong

A solar-powered temperature and humidity sensor in the garden writes poems from public-domain poetry by Black poets. It wakes with the light and writes throughout the day at the top of the hour; after dark, on its spent battery, it sleeps. Humidity sets the length; temperature sets the width and the wildness.
